# Compliance Rules for Financial / Insurance Documents
# Format: RULE_ID: Rule description
# Lines starting with # are comments and will be ignored

# --- Coverage & Policy Terms ---
RULE001: Document must explicitly state the coverage start and end date
RULE002: Policy must include the full legal name of the insured party
RULE003: Document must state the maximum liability or sum assured
RULE004: Premium payment terms and due dates must be specified
RULE005: Policy renewal terms must be clearly described

# --- Disclosures & Exclusions ---
RULE006: Document must disclose all exclusion clauses clearly
RULE007: Any pre-existing condition clauses must be explicitly mentioned
RULE008: Waiting period for claims must be stated if applicable

# --- Legal & Authentication ---
RULE009: Document must be signed or digitally authenticated by the issuing authority
RULE010: Policy issuance date must be present
RULE011: Unique policy or document reference number must be included

# --- Grievance & Cancellation ---
RULE012: Document must include a grievance redressal mechanism or contact information
RULE013: Cancellation terms and notice period must be present and explicitly described
RULE014: Refund or surrender value policy must be stated upon cancellation

# --- Regulatory ---
RULE015: Document must include the regulatory authority registration number of the insurer
